        Scoring & pot rules
        • Won exactly your bid: score = bid (bid 3, won 3 → +3.0)
        • Won more than your bid: bid + 0.1 per extra trick (bid 3, won 5 → +3.2)
        • Won fewer than your bid: score = −bid (bid 3, won 2 → −3.0)
        • Each round: total bids can't exceed 13, and total tricks won must equal exactly 13.
        • Base: what you receive from the pot minus what you pay into it, based on your final place (Fixed Pot) or points (Point-Based Pot).
        • Double penalty: a negative final total means you pay your pot share a second time. That money goes to 1st place, or first reduces what positive-scoring players owe — whichever the moderator chose.
        • Game length: the moderator sets the number of rounds, and can change it under Pot rules.
        • Tie for 1st: the moderator chooses to play a tie-breaker round (it counts toward everyone's totals) or to split the winner's money between the tied players.
        • Ties for lower places are separated by the final-round score, then the round before, and so on.
        • Settlement uses the fewest possible transfers.
